About PAMANA

PAMANA is a digital journalism portal created by 4th-year Journalism students from Bicol University. Our initiative aims to preserve, document, and promote the rich cultural heritage, traditions, historical sites, and environmental advocacies of the Bicol region.

×

Bicol Culture

Explore featured Bicolano cultural traditions and culinary heritage:

  • Pantomina: Traditional courtship dance of Bicol.
  • Ibalong Epic: Legendary tales and mythology of Bicolano heroes.
  • Culinary Delights: Sili Ice Cream, Pili delicacies, and Bicol Express.

Bicol Heritage

Discover historical landmarks and ancestral sites across Bicol:

  • Museo de Legazpi: Artifacts & local historical archives.
  • Camalig Ancestral Homes: Preserved Spanish-era architecture.
  • St. John the Baptist Parish Church: Historical century-old parish.

Bicol Environment

Read stories and initiatives on environmental conservation in Bicol:

  • Basura Mo, Bigas Mo: Community solid-waste management initiative.
  • Tree Planting Drives: Reforestation programs in Albay.
  • Local Wildlife: Albay Park and Wildlife Sanctuary conservation.
